FFmpeg notice for CD Explorer
=============================

This application bundles FFmpeg and FFprobe 9.0.2, obtained from the official
FFmpeg source distribution at https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz

Source archive SHA-256:
8c3850283eb25fa026482078a04051e0be17347b09ef81a0849bec15a96e002e

The binaries are built separately for arm64 and x86_64 and combined as
universal Mach-O executables. They include the GPL-licensed x264 encoder so
high-quality software H.264 conversion remains available even when macOS's
VideoToolbox encoder is unavailable.

x264 source revision 0480cb05fa188d37ae87e8f4fd8f1aea3711f7ee:
https://code.videolan.org/videolan/x264/-/archive/0480cb05fa188d37ae87e8f4fd8f1aea3711f7ee/x264-0480cb05fa188d37ae87e8f4fd8f1aea3711f7ee.tar.gz
Source archive SHA-256:
d0967a1348c85dfde363bb52610403be898171493100561efa0dd05d5fd1ae50

Notable configure options:
  --disable-shared --enable-static --disable-doc --disable-debug
  --disable-ffplay --disable-network --disable-autodetect
  --enable-zlib --enable-videotoolbox --enable-audiotoolbox
  --enable-gpl --enable-libx264
  --enable-ffmpeg --enable-ffprobe

the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later. A copy of version 2
accompanies these binaries. The corresponding unmodified sources are
